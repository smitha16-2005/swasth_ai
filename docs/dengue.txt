DENGUE FEVER - CLINICAL GUIDELINES (ICMR/WHO)

DEFINITION:
Dengue is a mosquito-borne viral infection causing severe flu-like illness.

SYMPTOMS:
- Sudden high fever (38-40°C)
- Severe headache
- Pain behind eyes
- Joint and muscle pain
- Nausea and vomiting
- Skin rash appearing 2-5 days after fever
- Mild bleeding from nose or gums

WARNING SIGNS (Severe Dengue - Immediate Hospital):
- Severe abdominal pain
- Persistent vomiting
- Bleeding from gums or nose
- Blood in vomit or stool
- Rapid breathing
- Fatigue and restlessness
- Liver enlargement

RISK CLASSIFICATION:
RED - severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding, difficulty breathing
YELLOW - fever more than 3 days, joint pain, rash, no warning signs
GREEN - mild fever less than 2 days, no warning signs

TREATMENT PROTOCOL:
- No specific antiviral treatment
- Paracetamol for fever (avoid aspirin and ibuprofen)
- Oral rehydration - minimum 2 litres per day
- Rest and monitor
- Platelet transfusion only if below 10,000

WHEN TO REFER:
Immediately refer to hospital if any warning signs appear.
